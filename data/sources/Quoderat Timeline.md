How to Read the Tower
Quoderat's history is an upside-down tower. You enter at the bottom, in the oldest age, and each floor up is a later event, until the top floor reaches the present. Floors are separated by events, not by fixed spans of time, and only world-changing events get a floor.
The Floors
Floor 0: Creation and the Age of Creatures
No god made the world. The laws always existed, waiting to be discovered, and the world itself condensed raw power into living vessels: the first creatures. Creation never ended. The world still condenses power today, only into totems for mortals instead of into dragons. Every race tells its own creation myth, and none can be proven:
Dragons: the world's first thought was matter, and dragons were that thought given wings. Everything after them is leftovers.
Unicorns: the world was wounded from the start and made unicorns to heal it. Life is the world's purpose.
Phoenixes: they have no myth. They forgot it, and say only that the world began, the way they always begin again.
Kitsune: creation was a bargain. Raw power traded itself for shape, and someone still owes on the deal.
Trolls: the world is a body, the mountains its bones, and trolls its strength. Their version is carved in stone and does not change.
Mortals: the world was a locked book, and the laws were written so that someone would one day read them.
No laws are held. Mortals live on Llama, a separate continent cut off by the Limitrest Range, whose highest peak is Mt Limitrest, as a scientific civilization with no power of any kind. 
Floor I: The Axiom
The first law holder holds the Law of All Laws. He discovers that mortals can wield laws and condenses that knowledge into the Axiom, a cheap bracelet whose instructions he releases openly. His test is breaking the Axiom. He was not among the ageless law holders and died a mortal's death; no one has scored a point since.
Floor II: The First Gods
Laws are discovered through the Axiom. Number gains its first holder, and among the later of the earliest gods, S&S discovers Non-Contradiction while his best friend founds the dynasty that will one day produce Tertia. New mortal kingdoms form around these first gods.
Floor III: The Great Hunt
Mortals cross the Limitrest Range into Origo, the creatures' continent, and law holders hunt every creature family out of greed, hoping to take their power, and learn too late that laws and physical power are mutually exclusive. Since law holders can't wield physical power, mortal armies do the actual hunting, paid in spoils: creature skin, scales and bone, which aren't magical but are exceptional materials. The power soaks into the world, which begins condensing it back out as totems.
* Unicorns are hunted hardest, for their horns as well as their power.
* Phoenixes keep returning, smaller and without their memories.
* Trolls are caught by daylight and petrified, their power locked in stone.
* Kitsune survive by hiding among mortals.
* Dragons survive, but lose their monopoly on matter.
Floor IV: The First Totems
For the first time, ordinary mortals receive power. The world hands out totems, and the strongest holders become a new elite powerful enough to challenge kings. Kingdoms and empires rise around them, ruling beneath the gods and creatures.
Floor V: The Feud
The war between Number and Sets, in four acts:
1. The demotion. Sets is discovered, and Number becomes its sublaw overnight. Sets' first holder then murders Number's first holder by hand.
2. The paradox. Ilar Hesabu, a descendant of the murdered holder, wins Number's test with answers leaked by his family. He finds a paradox in the original Sets and kills its arrogant first holder with one stroke after another. Number expands back into its lost status; Ilar assumes Sets is finished and flees to the earth dragons. Ever since, every holder keeps their law clean.
3. The long vacancy. Sets sits empty for generations, a zero-zero tie no one can break.
4. The restoration. Ensa wins Sets' test, the first to score in generations, and revises the law so the paradox can no longer be stated. Sets rises above Number again, quietly undoing Ilar's revenge.
Floor VI: The Present
Mortals live on both Llama and Origo. The Law of All Laws is vacant and the Axiom sits on every wrist; no one has ever scored a point. The kitsune sell cloaks to anyone who can pay. Most dragons seek coexistence, while the earth tribe quietly shelters Ilar Hesabu.

如何阅读这座塔
证毕的历史是一座倒置的塔。你从底层进入，那里是最古老的时代；每向上一层，便是更晚发生的事件，直到顶层抵达当下。楼层以事件划分，而非固定的时间跨度，只有改变世界的事件才能占据一层。
楼层
第零层：创世与神兽时代
世界并非由神所造。法则自古存在，等待被发现；世界自身将原始的力量凝聚成有生命的容器，即最初的神兽。创世从未结束。时至今日，世界仍在凝聚力量，只是如今凝成的是赐予凡人的图腾，而非龙族。每个种族都有自己的创世神话，却没有一个能被证明：
龙族：世界最初的念头是物质，而龙族就是那个念头生出了翅膀。此后的一切，不过是残余。
独角兽：世界生来便带着伤口，于是造出独角兽来治愈它。生命，就是世界的目的。
不死鸟：它们没有神话。或者说它们早已忘记。世界开始了，就像它们总会重新开始一样。
狐仙：创世是一笔交易。原始的力量用自身换取了形体，而至今仍有人欠着这笔账。
巨魔：世界是一具躯体，群山是它的骨骼，巨魔是它的力量。它们的版本刻在石头上，永不改变。
凡人：世界是一本上了锁的书，而法则之所以被写下，就是为了有朝一日有人能读懂它们。
此时尚无人执掌法则。凡人生活在羊驼大陆，以极限山脉与外界相隔，山脉最高峰为极限峰；他们是一个科学文明，不具任何力量。
第一层：公理
第一位执律者执掌万法之法。他发现凡人可以驾驭法则，并将这一知识凝结为公理：一只廉价的手环，其制作方法被他公开发布。他的试炼，便是破解公理。他并非不老的执律者，最终以凡人之躯死去；此后再无人得过一分。
第二层：最初的神
法则通过公理被发现。数获得了第一位执律者；在最早一批神之中稍晚出现的矛盾发现了不矛盾律，而他的挚友则建立了一个围绕着排中律的王朝，日后将诞生特尔提娅。新的凡人王国围绕这些最初的神而建立。
第三层：大狩猎
凡人翻越极限山脉，进入神兽的大陆原点。执律者出于贪婪猎杀每一个神兽族群，妄图夺取它们的力量，却为时已晚地发现法则与物理之力互不相容。由于执律者无法驾驭物理之力，真正动手狩猎的是凡人的军队，他们的报酬是战利品：神兽的皮、鳞与骨。这些并无魔力，却是非凡的材料。力量渗入世界，世界开始将其重新凝聚为图腾。
* 独角兽遭到最残酷的猎杀，人们既要它们的力量，也要它们的角。
* 不死鸟一次次归来，却越来越小，也失去了记忆。
* 巨魔被日光捕获而石化，力量被封锁在石中。
* 狐仙藏身于凡人之中得以幸存。
* 龙族得以幸存，却失去了对物质的垄断。
第四层：最初的图腾
普通凡人第一次获得了力量。世界开始发放图腾，最强的持有者成为新的精英，强大到足以挑战君王。王国与帝国围绕他们兴起，统治于神与神兽之下。
第五层：宿怨
数与集合之间的战争，共分四幕：
1. 降格。集合被发现，数一夜之间沦为其子法则。随后，集合的第一位执律者亲手杀害了数的第一位执律者。
2. 悖论。被害执律者的后裔伊拉尔·赫萨布凭借家族泄露的答案赢得了数的试炼。他在最初的集合中找到一个悖论，接连两击，击败并杀死了那位傲慢的集合第一位执律者。数重新扩张，夺回失去的地位；伊拉尔以为集合已经终结，逃往土龙族。从此以后，每位执律者都谨守自己法则的严密无瑕。
3. 漫长的空缺。集合空置了数代人之久，陷入无人能打破的零比零僵局。
4. 复原。恩莎赢得集合的试炼，成为数代以来第一个得分之人，并修订法则，使悖论再也无法被表述。集合再次凌驾于数之上，悄然抹去了伊拉尔的复仇。
第六层：当下
凡人如今同时生活在羊驼与原点两片大陆。万法之法依然空缺，公理戴在每个人的手腕上；从未有人得过一分。狐仙向任何付得起钱的人出售隐匿之力。多数龙族寻求共存，而土龙族则悄悄庇护着伊拉尔·赫萨布。

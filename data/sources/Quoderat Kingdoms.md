Two Ways to Rule
Mortals live under two systems. Llama is one federation, the Llama Concord, and Origo is a continent of kingdoms and empires. Power decides status on both, but it is spent differently.
The Llama Concord
* Origin: Llama’s first kingdoms formed around the first gods. Once the First Totems made every throne vulnerable to the new elite, Llama’s kings pooled their armies and courts into one federation, so that no single elite could ever take power alone. Ensa, sought out whenever a treaty needs a foundation, wrote its charter.
* The refusers: the kings who would not sign took their courts and loyalists over the Limitrest Range and founded the first kingdoms of Origo, beside the descendants of the Hunt’s veterans.
* Government: an elected assembly. Llama has no kings.
* Character: it is still the scientific civilization the mortals began as. Tech companies, the internet and mobile games all come from Llama.
The Kingdoms of Origo
* Courts: at the top they resemble modern Gulf monarchies, but less modern. Each is a state run by an imperial court.
* Kings: mortals with some power. A king holds totems, but weak ones: enough to keep control, never enough to rule by force alone. That is why armies exist.
* Backers: most kingdoms live off a backer, whether elites, a law holder or a creature family. Some stand alone.
* War: human nature being what it is, the kingdoms fight each other.
Four Powers of Origo
* Hadrek (backed by elites): a walled military kingdom on the border of the plasma dragons’ lands, which lie closest to mortal settlements and were hit hardest in the Hunt. Hadrek lives on permanent watch and pays elites well.
* Velmarion (backed by a law holder): an old empire obsessed with succession and bloodlines. Zaid is its kingmaker, and his testimony is worth more there than an army.
* Nessaline (backed by creatures): a trading coast built around the kitsune markets. It buys and sells cloaks, so its spies are the best on Origo and its borders the hardest to read.
* Kaldrun (backed by no one): a small kingdom that fights for itself. Creatures go deep and mortals go wide: almost every citizen trains several weak totems, so the whole country is a citizen army, too costly to conquer and not rich enough to be worth it. It can refuse conquest but never conquer. Its crown goes to its best citizen-soldier, and it looks down on kingdoms that rent their strength.
Elites
* Grades: the Axiom reads a totem’s depth and grades it. B is 20–25, A is 26–30, S is 31–35 and SS is 36–39. Elites are S and above. A hybrid’s half-blood (40–60) and a full creature (90–100) lie beyond the grades.
* The depth reader: the Axiom was built before totems existed. Once they appeared, Llama’s engineers added a depth reader to its open instructions, and the update spread to every wrist. Scholars still argue whether the Axiom simply accepted it, or whether the first holder built it ready for totems that did not exist yet.
* Practice: the deeper a totem, the more practice it takes to wield. Holders of weak totems have time to spare and fight for power; elites must stay free to practise.
* Why elites are never kings: to an elite, the totem matters more than a crown. Kingship would cost the practice their power depends on, so they serve courts and gods as hired professionals instead.
To Settle

* ☐ Other kingdoms and empires of Origo beyond these four.

两种统治
凡人生活在两种体制之下。羊驼是一个联邦，即羊驼协约联邦；原点则是王国与帝国林立的大陆。在两片大陆上，力量都决定地位，但用法不同。
羊驼协约联邦
* 起源：羊驼最早的王国围绕最初的神而建立。最初的图腾出现后，每一个王座都可能被新精英推翻，于是羊驼的君王们把军队与宫廷合并为一个联邦，使任何一位精英都无法独自夺权。每逢条约需要根基便会被请去的恩莎，为它起草了宪章。
* 拒签者：不愿签约的君王带着宫廷与追随者翻越极限山脉，在大狩猎老兵的后代身旁，建立了原点最早的王国。
* 政体：由选举产生的议会治理。羊驼没有君王。
* 特质：它仍是凡人最初的科学文明。科技公司、互联网与手机游戏都出自羊驼。
原点诸王国
* 宫廷：顶层类似现代海湾君主国，但没有那么现代。每个王国都是由帝国式宫廷治理的国家。
* 君王：拥有一定力量的凡人。君王持有图腾，但都是弱小的图腾：足以维持掌控，却永远不足以单凭武力统治。这就是军队存在的原因。
* 靠山：大多数王国依靠一个靠山而存续，可能是精英、执律者或某个神兽族群。也有些王国独自立足。
* 战争：人性使然，各王国彼此征战。
原点四强
* 哈德雷克（以精英为靠山）：一个筑有城墙的军事王国，位于等离子龙族领地的边境。那片领地离凡人聚居地最近，在大狩猎中受创最重。哈德雷克常年戒备，并以重金雇用精英。
* 维尔玛里昂（以执律者为靠山）：一个执着于继承与血统的古老帝国。扎伊德是它的造王者，他的证词在那里比一支军队更有分量。
* 内萨琳（以神兽为靠山）：一个围绕狐仙集市建立的贸易海岸。它买卖隐匿之力，所以它的间谍是原点最出色的，它的边界也最难看清。
* 卡尔德伦（没有靠山）：一个为自己而战的小王国。神兽走向深，凡人走向广：几乎每个公民都修炼多个弱小的图腾，于是整个国家就是一支公民军队，征服它代价太高，又不够富裕，不值得征服。它能拒绝被征服，却永远无法征服别人。它的王冠归于最出色的公民战士，它也看不起那些租用力量的王国。
精英
* 等级：公理读取图腾的深度并为其评级。B为20至25，A为26至30，S为31至35，SS为36至39。S级及以上即为精英。混血种的半血（40至60）与完整神兽（90至100）都在等级之外。
* 深度读取器：公理建成于图腾出现之前。图腾出现后，羊驼的工程师在公理公开的制作方法中加入了深度读取器，这一更新随即传遍每一只手腕。学者们至今仍在争论：公理只是接受了这次更新，还是第一位执律者早已为尚不存在的图腾做好了准备。
* 修炼：图腾越深，驾驭它所需的修炼就越多。弱小图腾的持有者有余暇争夺权力；精英则必须保持自由，以便修炼。
* 精英为何从不称王：对精英而言，图腾比王冠更重要。称王会夺走他们力量所依赖的修炼，所以他们以受雇的专业人士身份为宫廷与诸神效力。
